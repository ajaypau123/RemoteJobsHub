Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RemoteJobsHub ("we", "our", or "us"). RemoteJobsHub is an open job directory and search aggregator indexing direct company career portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday). We respect candidate privacy and are committed to protecting the limited personal information you share with us.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection:

  • Email Address: Only collected when you explicitly opt in to receive our free Monday Digest or VIP Job Alerts.
  • Search Preferences: Target role (e.g. Software Engineer, QA, DevOps), location scope, and optional keywords to customize your alert feed.
  • Application Tracking: Saved and applied jobs stored locally in your browser's localStorage (not transmitted to our servers).
  • Anonymized Usage Data: Privacy-friendly aggregate page views to understand popular search terms. We do not track sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address exclusively to:

  • Dispatch curated, verified direct-ATS job openings matching your preferences.
  • Deliver subscription receipts and expiration notices for paid Sprint Passes.
  • Zero Spam Policy: We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third-party advertisers or recruitment agencies.

4. Payment Information

All paid passes ($5 VIP Alerts, $10 Sprint Pass, $49 Recruiter Featured Pin) are processed by certified third-party payment gateways (such as Stripe). RemoteJobsHub never accesses, processes, or stores your full credit card number or bank details.

5. One-Click Unsubscribe & Erasure (GDPR / CCPA)

You have full control over your data. Every single email sent by RemoteJobsHub contains an instant, 1-click unsubscribe link. Upon unsubscribing, your alert dispatch is immediately halted and your record is marked inactive. You may also request complete erasure of your subscriber record by emailing support@remotejobshub.com.

6. Third-Party Links & ATS Portals

RemoteJobsHub provides direct application links to official company ATS platforms (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday). Once you navigate to an employer's portal or partner service, their respective privacy policies govern your job application submission.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at: support@remotejobshub.com.
