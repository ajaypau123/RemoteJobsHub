Why We Built JobsForIndia

“Like thousands of tech job seekers across India, we got tired of seeing fake consultancy jobs, ghost listings, and scam calls on traditional job portals asking candidates for registration fees.”

JobsForIndia was built by tech engineers as an automated, scam-free engine that indexes ONLY direct company career sites (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and verified company portals).

We will never charge you to apply, and we will never sell your resume or phone number to consultancies. Every link takes you straight to the employer's official ATS application page.
